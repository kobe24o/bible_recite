# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 7 日  
生效日期：2026 年 9 月 7 日

“圣经背诵助手”（下称“本应用”）重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用处理信息的方式。

## 1. 我们不会收集你的个人信息

本应用不提供账号注册或登录，不使用广告或分析 SDK，也不会向开发者服务器上传你的个人信息、学习记录、背诵成绩或录音。

经文浏览、搜索、背诵计划、艾宾浩斯复习、学习统计和答题记录均保存在你的设备本地。卸载本应用或清除应用数据可能会删除这些本地数据。

## 2. 麦克风和语音识别

当你主动开始背诵或答题录音时，本应用会请求并使用麦克风权限。音频流仅用于设备本机的 sherpa-onnx 语音识别和背诵评分：不会上传至开发者，也不会保存为录音文件。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭麦克风权限；关闭后，语音背诵和语音答题功能不可用，其他功能仍可使用。

## 3. 可选的答题模型服务

本应用的“答题模型”功能是可选功能，默认关闭。只有当你主动填写第三方 OpenAI 兼容服务的接口地址、模型名称和 API Key 并启用该功能时，本应用才会通过 HTTPS 向你选择的服务商发送为生成答题所必需的经文文本和请求指令。API Key 会存储在你的设备本地，并随请求发送给你选择的服务商；开发者不会接收该 API Key。

第三方服务商会按照其自身隐私政策处理收到的信息。请在启用前确认你同意该服务商的条款和隐私政策；你可以随时在“我的 → 答题模型”中关闭功能或删除配置。

## 4. 联网内容

本应用可在你主动同步时从公开网络地址下载预置背诵计划和共享答题题库。该过程不上传你的学习记录、录音或 API Key。网络服务可能会按其自身规则处理常规连接信息（如 IP 地址）；本应用不控制该处理。

## 5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集个人信息。由于本应用不提供账号系统或社交、聊天、用户发布内容功能，开发者不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

## 6. 政策更新与联系我们

若本政策发生实质性变化，我们会在本页面更新“更新日期”。如对本政策有疑问，请联系：<mingmingchen1989@gmail.com>。

# Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: September 7, 2026  
Effective date: September 7, 2026

Bible Recitation Assistant (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

## 1. No collection of personal information

The App has no account registration or sign-in, uses no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not upload personal information, learning records, recitation results, or recordings to the developer’s servers.

Scripture browsing and search, memorization plans, spaced-repetition reviews, learning statistics, and quiz records are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove this local data.

## 2. Microphone and speech recognition

When you choose to start a recitation or quiz recording, the App requests and uses microphone permission. Audio streams are used only for on-device sherpa-onnx speech recognition and scoring. They are not uploaded to the developer and are not saved as audio files. You may disable microphone permission in your system settings at any time; voice recitation and voice quiz features will then be unavailable while other features remain available.

## 3. Optional quiz model service

The App’s quiz-model feature is optional and disabled by default. Only if you choose to enter an OpenAI-compatible third-party service endpoint, model name, and API Key and enable the feature will the App send the scripture text and instructions needed to generate quiz questions to that provider over HTTPS. The API Key is stored locally on your device and is sent only to the provider you select; the developer does not receive it.

Third-party providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Please review and accept the provider’s terms and privacy policy before enabling this feature. You may turn it off or remove its configuration at any time in My → Quiz Model.

## 4. Online content

When you choose to synchronize, the App may download preset memorization plans and a shared quiz bank from public network addresses. This does not upload learning records, recordings, or API Keys. Network providers may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, under their own policies; the App does not control that processing.

## 5. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no account system, social features, chat, or user-generated-content features.

## 6. Updates and contact

If this policy changes materially, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. For questions about this policy, contact <mingmingchen1989@gmail.com>.
